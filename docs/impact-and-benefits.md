# Impact and Benefits

## Potential Benefits

- Improved situational awareness at zone level
- Adaptive guidance over static one-direction signage
- Better resilience via offline-first fallback concepts
- Retrofit orientation for existing building stock

## Stakeholder Value

- **Occupants:** clearer route guidance under dynamic conditions
- **Safety teams:** dashboard visibility and event timelines
- **Institutions:** phased modernization pathway for evacuation intelligence

## Social Relevance

The concept aligns with disaster management priorities where actionable local intelligence can reduce confusion during emergency response.

## Important Scope Note

Current impact claims are conceptual/prototyping-stage and require controlled empirical validation before operational adoption.
